Notifications & Settings

🔔

The bell icon (top-right) shows your unread notifications

•

Rating notifications — when your procedure/presentation is rated (shows label: Excellent, Good, etc.)

•

Assignment notifications — when a presentation is assigned to you

•

General comments — when a supervisor leaves a comment on your profile

•

Detachment verification — when your detachment logs are verified

⚙️ Settings: From the menu → Settings, you can change your password, upload a profile picture, and export your data.
⏰ Auto-expire: Notifications automatically expire after 48 hours.
