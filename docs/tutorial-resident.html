Install the App & Sign In

a

Open your mobile browser and go to scalpeldiary.com. You will see the landing page with an "Install App" prompt at the bottom.

b

Tap "Install" to add ScalpelDiary to your home screen. This makes it work like a native app — you can open it directly from your phone.

c

Go to your home screen, find the ScalpelDiary icon, and tap to open it.

d

Tap "Get Started" or "Sign In" and enter the email and password provided by your administrator.

e

When prompted, tap "Allow" to enable notifications — this ensures you receive real-time alerts when your procedures are rated.

Landing page with Install App prompt
Allow notifications to stay updated
💡 Tip: If you miss the install prompt, open your browser menu (⋮) → "Add to Home Screen".

Adding a Surgical Procedure

1

Tap the ☰ menu icon → Select "Add Log"

2

Fill in Patient Information: Date, MRN, Age, Sex, Diagnosis

3

Select Procedure Type (Elective / Semi-Elective / Emergency) and Institution

4

Enter the Procedure name, select Category and your Role (Primary Surgeon, 1st Assistant, etc.)

5

Select your Supervisor — only senior residents and actual supervisors appear in the list

6

Tap "Create Log" to submit

Menu → Add Log
💡 Multiple Procedures: You can add multiple procedures for the same patient by tapping "Add Another Procedure" — they share the same patient info and supervisor.

Adding a Presentation

1

Tap ☰ menu → "Presentations"

2

Tap the "Add Presentation" button

3

Fill in: Date, Title, Venue, Type (Morning Presentation, Seminar, etc.), and select a Moderator

4

Tap "Create" to submit

Presentations page
📌 Assigned Presentations: Check the "Assigned" tab to see presentations assigned to you by supervisors or the chief resident. Tap "Mark as Presented" when you've completed it.

Rating Other Residents (Year 2+)

If you're Year 2 or above, junior residents may select you as their supervisor. You'll receive a notification when this happens.

1

Tap the notification or go to ☰ menu → "Logs to Rate"

2

Tap a procedure to open the rating form

3

Enter a Rating (0-100) based on:

  • Preparation of the patient
  • Scientific knowledge demonstrated
  • Surgical skill and technique
4

Add a comment if you have feedback, then tap "Submit"

Menu → Logs to Rate
⚠️ Rules: You can only rate residents in a lower year than yours. You cannot rate yourself or same-year residents.
7

Understanding Your Ratings

Once you submit a procedure or presentation, its status will be "Pending". The supervisor/moderator will be notified to rate and comment on it.

✏️

You can edit or delete procedures/presentations while they are in Pending status.

🔒

After they are rated, you can no longer edit or delete them.

👁️

You will not see your exact score. Instead, you'll see a rating label:

Excellent90 and above🟢
Good71 – 89🔵
Satisfactory50 – 70🟡
PoorBelow 50🔴
📊 Average Rating: Your average rating will only appear after you have 10+ verified procedures or 5+ verified presentations.

Logging Detachment Procedures & Presentations

When you are on detachment (rotation at an external site), the logging process is slightly different:

🏥 General Surgery at ALERT

•

If you work alone with your senior resident, they can evaluate you directly — select them as supervisor.

•

If you work with a detachment senior (external supervisor), choose the "ALERT Supervisor" option and type their name.

📋 After the Detachment Month

1

The status of detachment procedures/presentations will remain "Pending" (shown as N/A).

2

Export your detachment procedures and presentations as PDF (from Settings → Export).

3

Get the exported paper signed by the detachment department head.

4

Bring the signed paper along with your detachment evaluation form to the Postgraduate Coordinator's office within 30 days of detachment completion.

5

The Postgraduate Coordinator will verify your logs and the procedures/presentations will be marked as verified.

🔄 This applies to all detachments:

  • Orthopedics
  • Plastic Surgery
  • OBGYN at Abebech Gobena
  • CTS at TASH
  • ER (presentations)
  • Anesthesiology (presentations)
  • ICU (presentations)
  • Oncology (presentations)

Exporting Your Data as PDF

1

Go to ☰ menu → "Settings"

2

Scroll to "Export Report" → Tap "Export to PDF"

3

A filter popup appears — choose:

  • Year — select which year to export
  • Date range — Full Year, specific Month, or Custom date range
  • Category — filter by procedure category (optional)
  • Institution — filter by institution (optional)
  • Include analytics — adds stats summary to the report
4

Tap "Download PDF" — the professional report downloads to your device

Export filter popup
