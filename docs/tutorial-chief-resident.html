Accessing Chief Resident Features

Chief Resident features are accessed through a special toggle in the sidebar. You must be designated as a Chief Resident by the administrator.

1

Sign in with your resident credentials as usual

2

Tap the ☰ menu icon to open the sidebar

3

Look for the "Chief Resident" toggle at the top of the sidebar

4

Tap the toggle to enable Chief Resident mode — new menu items appear

5

You can switch back to regular resident mode anytime by toggling off

💡 Tip: When Chief Resident mode is on, you still have access to all regular resident features (Add Log, Presentations, Analytics, etc.).
⚠️ Note: If you don't see the toggle, contact your administrator to enable Chief Resident access for your account.

Tips & Best Practices

🗓️ Scheduling Workflow
1

Start with Yearly Rotations — Set up the full year rotation schedule first, as this is the foundation for all other scheduling

2

Then Monthly Duties — Assign daily duties for each month, considering the rotation assignments

3

Then Monthly Activities — Assign weekly activities that complement the rotation and duty schedule

4

Assign Presentations — Distribute presentation assignments evenly across residents

5

Export & Share — Export PDFs and share with the department at the start of each month

⚖️ Fair Distribution
📊

Check the duty count summary at the bottom of the Monthly Duties page to ensure fair distribution

🔄

Rotate weekend and holiday duties evenly — no resident should have significantly more weekend calls

📋

Use the History tab in Assign Presentations to track who has presented recently

⚠️ Common Pitfalls
⚠️ Double Booking: The system will warn you if you try to assign a resident to two duties on the same day. Check for conflicts before saving.
⚠️ Category Setup: Set up all rotation, duty, and activity categories BEFORE starting to create schedules. Adding categories later works but can be confusing.
💡 Remember: You still have all regular resident features available. Toggle Chief Resident mode off when you want to add your own logs and presentations.
📱 Desktop View: For complex scheduling tasks (especially yearly rotations), consider using a desktop browser for a wider view of the table.
