Accessing Chief Resident Features

Chief Resident features are accessed through a dedicated section in the sidebar menu, located below your regular resident menu items.

1

Sign in with your resident credentials as usual

2

Tap the ☰ menu icon to open the sidebar

3

Scroll down past the regular resident menu — you will see the Chief Resident section with scheduling and management options

4

The Chief Resident menu includes: Yearly Rotations, Monthly Duties, Monthly Activities, and Assign Presentations

Chief Resident menu in the sidebar
💡 Tip: If you don't see the Chief Resident section, contact your administrator to enable Chief Resident access for your account.
2

Yearly Rotation Scheduling

The Yearly Rotation page allows you to assign each resident to a department or detachment for every month of the academic year.

1

From the Chief Resident menu, tap "Yearly Rotations"

2

You'll see rotation categories listed. Go under the category you want and tap "Add Resident" to assign a resident to that rotation

3

Tap any cell in the table to assign or change a rotation for that resident and month

4

If you need a rotation category that doesn't exist yet, use the "Manage Categories" button to add new ones

Yearly Rotation — Assign departments per month
📋 Color Coding: Each rotation category has a unique color, making it easy to spot patterns and ensure balanced distribution across residents.
3

Monthly Duty Scheduling

The Monthly Duty page lets you assign daily duty programs for each resident using a calendar view.

1

From the Chief Resident menu, tap "Monthly Duties"

2

Select the month and year group to schedule

3

Tap any day on the calendar to open the assignment popup

4

Select the duty type and assign a resident to that day

5

Weekend days are highlighted — these typically need on-call assignments

Monthly Duties — Calendar view
💡 Tip: Duties are color-coded by type. You can assign multiple duties per day to different residents. Check the duty count summary at the bottom to ensure fair distribution.
4

Monthly Activity Scheduling

Monthly Activities define where each General Surgery resident at Y12HMC spends their time during the week — OPD, Ward Round, OR, or Minor OR.

1

From the Chief Resident menu, tap "Monthly Activities"

2

Select the month and year group

3

Tap any cell to assign or change the activity for that resident and week

4

Activities are color-coded for easy visual identification

Monthly Activities — Weekly assignments
📌 Activities vs. Duties: Activities represent the primary weekly assignment (where the resident spends most of their time), while duties are specific daily tasks.
5

Assign Presentations

You can assign presentations to any resident — seminars, short presentations, morning presentations, or other formats.

1

From the Chief Resident menu, tap "Assign Presentations"

2

Select a resident, enter the presentation title, date, type, and venue

3

Tap "Assign Presentation" — the resident will receive a notification about the assignment

4

You can track the status of all assigned presentations on this page. Once the resident has presented, a supervisor can rate the presentation.

Assign Presentations to residents
6

Exporting Schedules as PDF

Each scheduling page (Yearly Rotations, Monthly Duties, and Monthly Activities) has an Export PDF button.

1

Navigate to the schedule you want to export

2

Tap the "Export PDF" button at the bottom of the page

3

The PDF downloads to your device — it includes all color coding and is formatted for A4 printing

4

Take a cropped screenshot of the PDF or share the file directly with the department

💡 Tip: Export PDFs at the beginning of each month and share them with the department. The exported schedules are well-organized and ready for printing or digital distribution.
📱 Desktop View: For complex scheduling tasks (especially yearly rotations), consider using a desktop browser for a wider view of the table.
