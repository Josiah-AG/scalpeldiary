General Comments

General Comments let you leave feedback on a resident's profile that isn't tied to a specific procedure or presentation.

1

Tap ☰ menu → "General Comments"

2

Select a resident from the dropdown

3

Type your comment and tap "Submit Comment"

4

The resident will receive a notification about the new comment

💡 Tip: General comments appear on the resident's profile and in their "All Comments" page. Use them for overall performance feedback, not procedure-specific notes.
⚠️ Note: General comments are visible to the resident. For private feedback, use the anonymous comment field when rating a specific procedure.
