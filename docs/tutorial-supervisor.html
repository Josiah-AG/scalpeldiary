Install the App & Sign In

a

Open your mobile browser and go to scalpeldiary.com. You will see the landing page with an "Install App" prompt at the bottom.

b

Tap "Install" to add ScalpelDiary to your home screen. This makes it work like a native app — you can open it directly from your phone.

c

Go to your home screen, find the ScalpelDiary icon, and tap to open it.

d

Tap "Get Started" or "Sign In" and enter the email and password provided by your administrator.

e

When prompted, tap "Allow" to enable notifications — this ensures you receive real-time alerts when residents submit procedures or presentations for your review.

Set Up Your Profile

After logging in, go to Settings to personalize your account:

1

Tap the ☰ menu icon (top-left) → Select "Settings"

2

Upload your profile picture — tap the camera icon on your avatar

3

Change your password to something easily memorable — enter your current password, then your new one

3

Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard is your command center — it shows your metrics, today's duty residents, schedules, and quick access to all features.

At the top you'll see your pending ratings count, total procedures and presentations supervised, and today's duty residents. You can also view the monthly activity schedule showing which residents are on EOPD, Ward Round, or OR, and see which resident is currently at which rotation. Tap any card to view the full monthly schedule.

Below the metrics, you'll find year buttons (Year 1, 2, 3, 4). Tap any year to see all residents in that group with their procedure counts, presentation counts, and progress. Tap a resident card to view their full profile in read-only mode — you can see their dashboard, all procedures, presentations, analytics, and comments.

👁️ What you see that residents don't: Exact numeric scores (residents only see Excellent/Good/Satisfactory/Poor labels) and anonymous comments (yellow tag).

Rating Procedures (Unresponded Logs)

When a resident submits a procedure and selects you as their supervisor, you will receive a notification. The procedure appears in your Unresponded Logs.

1

Tap ☰ menu → "Unresponded Logs", or tap the Pending Ratings card on your dashboard

2

You'll see all procedures awaiting your review. Tap a procedure to open the rating form

3

Enter a Rating (0–100). When rating, consider:

  • Pre-operative preparation of the patient
  • Scientific knowledge and reading about the procedure
  • Surgical skill, technique, and communication
4

Add a comment if you wish — this will be visible to the resident and they will be notified

5

If you want to leave feedback that the resident should not see, use the "Make Anonymous Comment" option at the bottom of the rating modal. Only other supervisors and management can see anonymous comments.

6

Tap "Submit Rating"

⚠️ Important: Once submitted, ratings cannot be edited. Double-check your score and comments before submitting.

Rating Presentations

When a resident submits a presentation and selects you as moderator, you will be notified. Presentations also appear in Unresponded Logs under the "Presentations" tab.

1

From "Unresponded Logs", tap the "Presentations" tab

2

Tap a presentation to open the rating form

3

Enter a Rating (0–100). When rating presentations, consider:

  • Preparation and pre-presentation communication with you
  • Organization and presentation skill
  • Ability to respond to follow-up questions
4

Add a comment (visible to resident) and optionally an anonymous comment (hidden from resident)

5

Tap "Submit Rating". The resident will be notified of the rating.

📊 Presentation averages: Once a resident has 5+ verified presentations, their average presentation rating will appear on their dashboard.

Ratings Done & Post-Op Follow-Up

1

Tap ☰ menu → "Ratings Done"

2

Here you can see all the procedures and presentations you have previously rated

3

If you want to add a post-operative follow-up note — for example, to comment on the resident's post-op patient care — tap the procedure you rated before

4

Inside the procedure detail, you will find a Post-Op Follow-Up Comment section where you can leave your note

7

Assign Presentations

1

Tap ☰ menu → "Assign Presentations"

2

Select a resident from the dropdown, then fill in the presentation title, date, type (Morning Presentation, Seminar, Grand Round, etc.), and venue

3

Tap "Assign Presentation" — the resident will receive a notification about the assignment

4

You can track the status of assigned presentations on this page. Once the resident has presented, you will be able to rate the presentation from your Unresponded Logs

Assign Presentations to residents
💡 Tip: Assigned presentations appear in the resident's "Assigned" tab under Presentations. They can mark it as "Presented" once completed, and it will then appear in your Unresponded Logs for rating.
8

General Comments

General Comments allow you to leave feedback on a resident that is not tied to a specific procedure or presentation. Use this for comments on attitude, interaction, professionalism, or any other behavioral observation.

1

Tap ☰ menu → "General Comments"

2

Residents are listed by year (collapsible). Tap a year to expand, then select a resident

3

Type your comment and tap "Submit Comment"

4

By default, the comment is visible to the resident and they will be notified

5

If you want the comment to remain private, check the "Make Anonymous" checkbox before submitting. Anonymous comments are only visible to other supervisors and management — the resident will not see or be notified about them.

General Comments — Profile-level feedback
⚠️ Note: Anonymous general comments are tagged with a yellow "Anonymous" badge in the supervisor/management view. Residents never see anonymous content.
