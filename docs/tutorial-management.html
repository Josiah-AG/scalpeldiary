Viewing a Resident's Full Profile

When you tap a resident, you enter their profile in read-only mode. You can see everything they see, plus anonymous comments.

📊

Dashboard — Their procedures, presentations, calendar, today's overview

📋

All Procedures — Full list with filters, ratings shown as exact scores

📄

Presentations — All presentations with ratings

📈

4

Browsing Supervisors

You can also view statistics for all supervisors in the program.

1

This is accessible from the management dashboard if you have the management role

2

Each supervisor card shows: Unique Procedures Supervised, Presentations Supervised, Avg Rating Given

3

Tap a supervisor to see their rated procedures and presentations

📊 Unique Procedures: The count shows unique procedures (distinct MRN + date), not total log entries. One surgery with 3 residents counts as 1 procedure.

📊 Detachment ratings don't count toward the resident's average rating, but the procedures count as verified in their progress.
